Features

Everything kozzyx can do.

Designed to run a Discord server with as little hand-holding as possible.

Moderation

Full mod toolkit: warnings with strikes, mute / timeout, kick, ban, soft-ban, bulk-purge, and a per-user case history that's searchable from the dashboard. Auto-mod handles raid spikes, link blacklists, banned-word patterns, and account-age filters.

AI commands

Powered by Google Gemini. Members can /ask anything in channel, and staff can summarize threads, translate, or rewrite messages with a single command. Enable per-channel, cap usage, or turn it off entirely from Settings.

Tickets & appeals

Members open a ticket from a button; the bot spins up a private thread, pings the right staff role, and archives the whole log when it's resolved. Ban appeals get their own workflow with approval / denial right from the dashboard.

Server setup

/server_setup walks the owner through roles, channels, welcome message, and verification gate in under a minute. Autoresponders, scheduled messages, sticky notes, and reaction roles are all configurable from one place.

Members & leveling

XP per message, tunable cooldowns, role rewards at level thresholds, a public /leaderboard, and birthday tracking with channel announcements.

Plugins

Toggle whole feature sets on or off — anti-raid, giveaways, music, polls, reminders, modmail — without restarting the bot.

Dashboard

A real web dashboard: live activity feed, mod queue, AI config, leaderboard, and server settings, all editable in the browser.

Privacy & data

kozzyx only stores what's needed to run the features you've enabled. Full breakdown is in the privacy policy.
